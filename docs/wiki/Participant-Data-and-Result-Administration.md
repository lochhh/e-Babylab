The Participant Data Admin shows the list of participants in all experiments a user has access to. 

*Participant Data Admin*
![Participants list](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a7c1a058-c760-41db-8087-e0a9db92d054)

Here, users can view a participant's data, which includes the information provided in the participant form, their screen resolution, participant number, UUID (automatically generated; to distinguish participants from different experiments having the 
same participant number), participation date, experiment participated in, list assigned, CDI estimate, CDI item responses, and trial results. Users can also remove all of a participant's data and results by deleting the participant.
